The Practitioner

Dr. Sudhir Lohia

Homoeopath & Health Care Consultant

Dr. Sudhir Lohia operates Lohia's Homoeopathy as a dedicated clinical practice, offering individualized homeopathic consultations and balanced healthcare guidance.

Classical homeopathy centers on understanding the individual. Illness manifests differently in every patient based on bodily constitution, mental temperament, and environmental factors. Dr. Lohia takes the necessary time to observe this complete picture.

Every consultation is guided by foundational homeopathic principles. Remedies are selected according to individual totality and constitutional characteristics rather than generic, temporary symptom suppression.

An initial consultation with Dr. Lohia involves thorough evaluation of chief complaints, triggers, modalities, physical sensitivities, sleep patterns, and lifestyle stresses to determine the most suitable homeopathic guidance.

We operate with transparent medical ethics: setting realistic timelines, clarifying the course of treatment, and offering balanced dietary and daily lifestyle advice alongside prescribed remedies.
